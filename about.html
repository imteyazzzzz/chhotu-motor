OUR MISSION

NO RIDER SHOULD BE STRANDED ALONE

Kathmandu's roads are unpredictable — potholes, monsoon floods, and long distances between workshops. We built Chhotu around one idea: the workshop should come to the breakdown, not the other way around.

Whether it's a midnight roadside fix, a scheduled home visit, or a full overhaul at our Jorpati workshop, every job runs on genuine parts, transparent pricing, and mechanics who explain what they're doing and why.

Certified Mechanics
